# Visualization standards

Charts and other non-map visualizations must communicate the underlying evidence without obscuring its limitations.

- Use titles and labels that state the subject, units, and relevant time period.
- Choose a visual form suited to the comparison or relationship being shown.
- Identify data sources and disclose transformations, exclusions, and uncertainty.
- Use scales and baselines that do not exaggerate differences; make any non-zero baseline explicit.
- Maintain readable labels and contrast, and do not encode essential distinctions with color alone.
- Provide concise explanatory text or an equivalent description when publishing visualizations.

Shared color and typography should follow the semantic tokens in `design/tokens/` once established.
