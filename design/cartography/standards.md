# Cartographic standards

Maps must support interpretation of the research rather than decoration.

- State the mapped subject, geographic extent, time period, and units where relevant.
- Select and document a projection appropriate to the geographic extent and analytical purpose.
- Use a clear visual hierarchy, legible labels, and legends that explain symbols and units.
- Choose color scales appropriate to data type; do not imply order or magnitude where none exists.
- Show uncertainty, missingness, and important exclusions when they affect interpretation.
- Include source attribution and relevant methodological notes with each published map.
- Consider color-vision accessibility, contrast, and a non-color cue where practical.

There is no platform-wide default projection or palette until research contexts and the DESIGN token system support an explicit decision.
